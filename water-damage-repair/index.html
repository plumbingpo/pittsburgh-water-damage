Maintenance Tips for Water Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Do not paint or patch over a water stain until the source is fixed.
  • Once a year, renew the caulk at sinks, showers and tubs to keep water from entering the wall.
  • Save spare tile and flooring, plus paint color details, for matching later repairs.
  • Curing time for paint, floor adhesive and caulk should pass before the room sees water or hard use.
  • Use moisture resistant drywall and backer board when rebuilding bathrooms, kitchens and laundry rooms.
  • Before a contractor closes a floor or wall, request the final moisture numbers.

When to Call for Water Damage Repair in Pittsburgh

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • You see brown stains, peeling or bubbles in paint along the path the water took.
  • After drying, the hardwood is still cupped, crowned or lifting.
  • Interior doors now stick or will not latch after swelling from moisture.
  • Grout is breaking up and some tiles have lifted where moisture reached the floor below.
  • A leak overhead left the ceiling drywall cracked, sagging or falling apart.
  • Baseboards and door casings are warped or no longer sit flat against the wall.
  • During drying, sections of wallboard came out and the framing is still exposed.
