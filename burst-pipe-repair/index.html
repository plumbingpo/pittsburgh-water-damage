Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

When a pipe bursts in Pittsburgh or the surrounding area, Pittsburgh Restoration Co runs an emergency response around the clock. We can help find the shutoff and stop the water, and our team works with licensed plumbers to see that the pipe itself is repaired correctly. With the source under control, our crew goes straight to extraction, using truck-mounted machines and portable extractors to clear standing water from each affected area quickly.

After the water is out, we dry every material that took on moisture: finished floors, subflooring, wood framing, insulation and drywall. Burst pipes often damage multiple rooms and multiple levels, because water follows gravity through wall cavities, floor assemblies and ceiling spaces. Our thermal imaging and moisture detection equipment traces water migration paths so every affected area is identified and properly dried, which prevents the mold growth and structural deterioration that hidden moisture causes.

The Benefits of Professional Burst Pipe Repair

What you can see after a pipe bursts is usually only part of the damage. It moves between ceiling joists, inside floor assemblies, beside electrical conduit and down wall cavities, soaking materials you might not find for days or even weeks. By then, mold may already be established in hidden spaces and structural materials may be significantly weakened. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

Professional-grade extraction and drying equipment also works on a completely different scale than consumer tools. Truck-mounted extractors remove water hundreds of times faster than a shop vacuum, and commercial dehumidifiers pull 10 to 20 times more moisture from the air than residential units. With only 24 to 48 hours before mold starts, you need equipment this fast and this strong.

Signs You Need Burst Pipe Repair in Pittsburgh

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • In a cold snap, exposed lines that look frosted or frozen over
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
  • Damp, moldy odors around sinks, tubs or walls that hide pipes
  • Visible water spraying or dripping from exposed pipes
  • Puddles on the floor that you cannot trace to anything obvious
  • A stained, soggy or bowed section of drywall overhead or beside you
  • A fixture that stops flowing when it is below freezing outside

Burst Pipe Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • When it freezes, let taps drip and open the cabinets under sinks to let warm air reach the pipes
  • Leak detectors placed close to main supply lines and the water heater give early warning
  • Everyone in the house should be able to locate and turn the main water valve in a hurry
  • Pressure higher than 80 PSI wears on pipes and their fittings, so test yours from time to time
  • Leave the heat on when you travel in winter so indoor pipes stay above freezing
  • Every few years, ask a licensed plumber to look over the system for corrosion and worn parts
